titre: Programme de Bâle (premier congrès sioniste), traduction anglaise du Foreign Office, 1920
date: 30 août 1897
langue: anglais
source: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Zionism/The_Basel_Programme
---
Zionism strives to create for the Jewish people a home in Palestine secured by public law. The Congress contemplates the following means to the attainment of this end: The promotion on suitable lines of the colonization of Palestine by Jewish agricultural and industrial workers. The organization and binding together of the whole of Jewry by means of appropriate institutions. local and international, in accordance with the laws of each country. The strengthening and fostering of Jewish national sentiment and consciousness. Preparatory steps towards obtaining Government consent where necessary to the attainment of the aim of Zionism.
